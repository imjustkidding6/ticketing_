You can now send feedback without leaving the app. Look for the Feedback button at the bottom-right corner of any page.

What you can submit:
- Bug reports
- Suggestions
- Compliments
- General feedback

Every submission goes directly to our team. No action is required — just click the button whenever you have something to share.
